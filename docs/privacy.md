# Tele privacy policy

Tele is a remote control for Samsung TVs. It runs entirely on your iPhone and talks only to the
TVs you add, over your own Wi-Fi network.

**What Tele stores.** For each TV you add, Tele keeps its name, model, network address, MAC address
and the pairing token the TV issues when you allow Tele on its screen. This stays on your device,
in the app's own storage shared with its widget. Removing a TV deletes it.

**What Tele sends.** Key presses, app launch requests and status checks go directly to your TV over
the local network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. Tele has no account,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Permissions.** Tele asks for local network access so it can find and control your TV. It needs
nothing else.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
