# Tele support

**Requirements.** A Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or later) on the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone.

**The TV does not appear.** Make sure the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi, then tap *Scan again*.
You can also type the TV's address, shown on the TV under Settings > General > Network > Network
Status > IP Settings.

**The TV keeps asking to allow Tele.** Accept the prompt on the TV with its own remote while Tele is
open. If you removed Tele from the TV's device list (Settings > General > External Device Manager >
Device Connection Manager), use *Pair again* in Tele's settings.

**Power on does not work.** Enable *Power On with Mobile* on the TV (Settings > General > Network >
Expert Settings). Some TVs on Wi-Fi only wake within a few minutes of being turned off.

**An app tile does nothing.** The app is not installed on that TV, or the TV uses a different app
id. Open an issue with the TV model.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/willysvarela/tele/issues.
